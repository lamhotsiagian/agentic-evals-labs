# Global HR & Remote Work Policy (DOC-HR-101)
**Department**: Human Resources  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Document ID**: DOC-HR-101  

## 1. Remote Work Stipend & Equipment
Full-time remote and hybrid employees are eligible for:
* **One-time Home Office Equipment Reimbursement**: Up to **$500.00 USD** for ergonomic chairs, desks, monitors, or peripheral accessories upon hire.
* **Monthly Internet & Connectivity Stipend**: **$50.00 USD per month** ($600.00 annually) distributed directly through monthly payroll.
* **Hardware Refresh Cycle**: Company-provisioned laptops are eligible for standard hardware upgrade every 36 months.

## 2. Paid Time Off & Well-Being Days
Full-time employees accrue 20 standard PTO days annually, in addition to 11 recognized company holidays and 2 floating personal well-being recharge days.
