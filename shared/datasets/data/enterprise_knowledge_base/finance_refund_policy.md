# Customer Refund & Subscription Terms (DOC-FIN-303)
**Department**: Finance & Accounting  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Document ID**: DOC-FIN-303  

## 1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
* New subscriptions canceled within **30 calendar days** of initial purchase qualify for a **100% full money-back guarantee**.
* Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## 2. Exceptions & Prorated Refunds
* Cancellation requests submitted after the 30-day window are non-refundable by default.
* Prorated exceptions for enterprise accounts with custom contractual terms require written approval from the Department Director.
* Chargebacks initiated without contacting support incur a $25 merchant processing assessment fee.
